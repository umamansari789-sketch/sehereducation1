Important Fee Note

Please note that the tuition fee mentioned in the MBBS package covers only the academic instruction charges. In addition to tuition, private medical colleges generally levy several mandatory charges which are not included in the tuition fee. These may vary from college to college but typically include

  • Registration & Admission Charges
  • Examination Fees (annual/semester)
  • Library & Laboratory Fees
  • Hostel & Mess Charges (if applicable)
  • Clinical/Practical Training Fees
  • University Affiliation & Development Fees
  • Security Deposit / Caution Money(refundable in some cases)
  • Miscellaneous Charges (ID card, uniforms, medical insurance, etc.)
