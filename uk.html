Why Study in the Uzbekistan ?

Uzbekistan is a popular choice for MBBS due to its affordable tuition fees and low cost of living compared to many other countries. Medical universities here are recognized by NMC and WHO and offer programs in English, making it convenient for Indian students. Students also benefit from modern infrastructure, clinical exposure, and a safe, student-friendly environment.

MBBS in Uzbekistan typically spans 5–6 years, including academic learning and clinical training, making it a well-structured medical program. The overall cost is much more affordable compared to many other countries, with low tuition fees and living expenses. Graduates can pursue licensing exams like NEXT/FMGE and explore career opportunities in India or abroad after completing their degree.
